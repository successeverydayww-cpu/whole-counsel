The Whole Counsel

"For I have not shunned to declare unto you all the counsel of God."
— Acts 20:27
Finally read the whole Bible — Genesis to Revelation — without falling off.
🗺️

A track that carries you, not guilt

You have read books of the Bible before. What was missing was a system that keeps you consistent. The Whole Counsel gives you a fixed daily portion — 4 chapters by default, yours to change anytime — that carries you through all 66 books in under a year. Today's portion is always waiting on your home screen. You just open and read.

🎧

It reads the Word to you

Driving, walking, or resting your eyes? Press Listen and the app reads today's portion aloud, highlighting each verse as it goes — with speed control for your pace.

🔥

Streaks that encourage, not shame

Every chapter you complete is remembered. Your streak counts your faithfulness, your progress bar shows how far you have come, and if you miss a day — nothing breaks. You simply continue. Grace is built in.

🏺

The original words, in your pocket

Every word of Scripture is tappable. Touch any underlined word and the app opens its original Hebrew or Greek — the real word, its pronunciation, and its true meaning from the Strong's lexicon. Not a paraphrase. The origin. And when you want to go deeper, the Ask button brings a living teacher to any chapter or word.

🔤

A taste of what every verse opens up for members:

📚

A Christian library and your own AI Librarian

Members get a free library of classic Christian books and an AI Librarian: tell it any title, author or topic, and it searches the free libraries, finds the book in seconds and drops it into your My Books shelf to read anywhere, even offline.

🌱

Coming soon

Daily reminders, a journal and prayer notes, memory verses, a chronological reading plan, and deeper word studies across every book. The Whole Counsel keeps growing.

🤝

Read it together — fellowship built in

Create a fellowship, share the code, and your group reads the whole counsel together. See each other's progress and streaks — to spur one another on, never to shame. Perfect for a campus fellowship, a class, or a family.

📶

Works offline, installs like an app

Load it once and the Scriptures live on your phone — no network needed to read. Install it to your home screen and it opens full-screen, like a real app.

Terms & Privacy

Terms of Use

By using The Whole Counsel you agree to these terms. The app offers free daily Bible reading; Word Study, the e-book library, the AI Librarian, Viva the Bible teacher and Fellowship are membership features.

1. Your account

Keep your password safe. One account per person. You are responsible for what is posted in your fellowship: keep it loving, encouraging and true (Eph 4:29). Leaders may remove messages and members who misuse the fellowship.

2. Membership & payments

Memberships (₦1,100 for 3 months, ₦4,400 for 1 year) are paid by bank transfer to the account shown in the app and verified by our team, usually within minutes. Access lasts for the period paid for. Fees may change; a change never shortens a period you have already paid.

3. The books and teaching

The e-book library shares public-domain Christian classics — legal and free. The Librarian and Viva are AI study helpers: they work from the text, but they are helpers, not authorities; the Word itself is final. Books are for your personal reading only.

4. Fellowship conduct

No abuse, false teaching spread with intent, or illegal content. Groups belong to the community: a group is never deleted — if its owner leaves, a leader is appointed from the remaining members.

5. Service

We work to keep the app available, but we cannot promise uninterrupted service. If something breaks, tell us — we fix things fast.

Privacy Policy

We collect only what the app needs: your email (your login), your display name, your reading progress, your membership record, and the messages and videos you post in your fellowship. Reading is private; fellowship content is visible to your fellowship members.

What we never do

We never sell your data, never share your email with third parties, and never post on your behalf. Membership payments are verified by our team only to unlock your access.

Where data lives

Your account and progress are stored on our secure Cloudflare backend. Progress is also saved on your phone so you can read offline.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from Profile at any time. Your account, progress and fellowship messages are removed from our records. Fellowship groups remain for the members who stay.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages, but members under 13 should join with a parent or guardian's permission.
