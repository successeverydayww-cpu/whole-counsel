The Whole Counsel

"For I have not shunned to declare unto you all the counsel of God."
— Acts 20:27
Finally read the whole Bible — Genesis to Revelation — without falling off.
🗺️

A track that carries you, not guilt

You have read books of the Bible before. What was missing was a system that keeps you consistent. The Whole Counsel gives you a fixed daily portion — 4 chapters by default, yours to change anytime — that carries you through all 66 books in under a year. Today's portion is always waiting on your home screen. You just open and read.

🎧

It reads the Word to you

Driving, walking, or resting your eyes? Press Listen and the app reads today's portion aloud, highlighting each verse as it goes — with speed control for your pace.

🔥

Streaks that encourage, not shame

Every chapter you complete is remembered. Your streak counts your faithfulness, your progress bar shows how far you have come, and if you miss a day — nothing breaks. You simply continue. Grace is built in.

📶

Works offline, installs like an app

Load it once and the Scriptures live on your phone — no network needed to read. Install it to your home screen and it opens full-screen, like a real app.
